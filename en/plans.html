Workout splits

Choose your training track.

Use bodyweight or light home equipment, and build up gradually with your coach.

Full-body · 3 days

Full-body sessions

A flexible three-session starting plan with strength movements for the whole body.

  • Day AChair sit-to-stand 2–3 × 8–12 · Incline wall/table push-up 2–3 × 6–12 · Band/bag row 2–3 × 8–12 · Glute bridge 2–3 × 10–15 · Dead bug 2 × 6–10 each side
  • Day BSupported reverse lunge 2–3 × 6–10 each side · Light hip hinge 2–3 × 8–12 · Light shoulder press 2–3 × 8–12 · Band row 2–3 × 8–12 · Bird dog 2 × 6–10 each side
  • Day CLow step-up 2–3 × 8–10 each side · Incline push-up 2–3 × 6–12 · Glute bridge 2–3 × 10–15 · Bag row 2–3 × 8–12 · Side plank 2 × 15–25 seconds each side
Upper/lower · 4 days

Upper and lower body

Alternate upper-body and lower-body sessions, with recovery days throughout the week.

  • Upper AIncline push-up 3 × 6–12 · Band/bag row 3 × 8–12 · Light shoulder press 2 × 8–12 · Bird dog 2 × 6–10 each side
  • Lower AChair sit-to-stand 3 × 8–12 · Hip hinge 3 × 8–12 · Glute bridge 3 × 10–15 · Calf raise 2 × 10–15
  • Upper BIncline push-up 3 × 6–12 · Bag or band row 3 × 8–12 · Light lateral raise 2 × 10–15 · Comfortable core exercise for 2 rounds.
  • Lower BSit-to-stand or squat 3 × 8–12 · Supported reverse lunge 2 × 6–10 each side · Glute bridge 3 × 10–15 · Calf raise 2 × 10–15.

Sets and reps are general examples, not an individualized prescription. Start with suitable resistance and finish each set feeling you could still do a few good reps. Stop if you feel sharp pain and ask a qualified coach for guidance.

Progressive strength

Build muscle

Choose resistance that lets you keep good form. Track reps or loads to see progress.

  • Upper A: Incline push-up, bag/band row, shoulder press, core movement.
  • Lower A: Sit-to-stand, hip hinge, glute bridge.
  • Upper B + Lower B: Repeat push, pull, squat and lunge patterns with suitable variations.
  • Food: Eat varied meals with protein sources; ask a professional to estimate your needs instead of guessing calories.
Gradual progress & recovery

Gain weight

Combine strength sessions with regular meals. Discuss unintentional weight loss with a healthcare professional.

  • Full body A: Chair squat, wall push-up, band row, glute bridge.
  • Full body B: Supported lunge, hip hinge, light shoulder press, bird dog.
  • Full body C: Low step-up, incline push-up, bag row, side plank.
  • Food: Add a nourishing snack if needed; consult a dietitian, especially if you are underweight.
Strength + movement

Lose fat

Keep resistance training and everyday movement in your routine. Avoid crash diets or rapid changes.

  • Monday: Full body A, 2–3 sets at comfortable reps.
  • Wednesday: Full body B with rest as needed.
  • Saturday: Full body C; optional easy walks on two other days.
  • Food: Focus on balanced meals, vegetables and water; ask a professional for a personal plan if needed.
Consistency

Maintain fitness

Keep a routine you can repeat and swap days or exercises to fit your week.

  • Monday: Full body A.
  • Wednesday: Full body B or mobility.
  • Saturday: Full body C or an activity you enjoy.
  • Food: Keep variety in your meals; you do not need to change weight just to reach a BMI number.
